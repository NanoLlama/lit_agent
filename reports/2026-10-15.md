## Top picks (score 4-5)

### Shuttle receptors and targeting

#### Bispecific antibodies that engage pIgR enrich in mucosa in cynomolgus monkeys
[PMID:42829333](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42829333/) · MAbs · 2026-10-03

pIgR-targeted bispecifics were tested in cynomolgus monkeys after showing that pIgR expression in cyno resembles human (RNA and protein across tissues). They cleared rapidly from serum and enriched 5-10-fold in bronchoalveolar lavage fluid compared to normal IgG antibodies. The measurement is in lavage fluid, not tissue. Gastrointestinal mucosa was also evaluated, but no GI number is reported in the abstract.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** transit. This is a non-human-primate readout that a transcytosing epithelial receptor moves IgG-format cargo from blood into a barrier-protected lumen, and fast serum depletion is the PK cost to plan around.

#### NGS-Guided Discovery of Cross-Reactive TfR1 VHH Antibodies with Distinct Brain Localization Profiles
[PMID:42855849](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42855849/) · J Biochem · 2026-10-10

Phage display plus NGS-guided selection of sequence-diverse clones from immunized alpacas gave three human/mouse cross-reactive TfR1 VHH-Fc clones (A1, Y11, Z1). The abstract reports ELISA EC50 ">100 nM" for all three. After IV dosing in mice, Y11 showed brain-associated fluorescence out to 48 h, while A1 started high and declined. Y11 localized to NeuN-positive neuronal regions and A1 to lectin-positive vasculature.

**Evidence level:** fluorescence imaging and microscopy only. No quantitation is reported in the abstract (no %ID/g, no parenchyma-to-capillary ratio, no cargo). The abstract does not show measured brain delivery, and vascular vs. parenchymal signal is the open question.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** transit. The Y11 vs. A1 split (neuronal vs. vascular, sustained vs. declining) suggests clone-level differences in what happens after TfR1 binding, but that needs quantitative follow-up before it counts as a delivery gain.

### Conjugation and loading

#### Development of a Polypeptide-Based Platform to Construct Antibody-Drug Conjugates with High Potency
[PMID:42819270](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42819270/) · JACS Au · **published 2026-08-14 (before the window; indexed in it)**

A click-chemistry polypeptide backbone carries many payloads per antibody. Lead ADC 6c reached a DAR of up to 61. It still recognized antigen, was endocytosed, and showed subnanomolar activity in cells. The abstract also describes "excellent" PK and potent antitumor efficacy in mice, but gives no PK parameters, dosing, or tumor-response numbers ("not reported in abstract").

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** loading. DAR 61 is far beyond conventional DAR 2-8, and the abstract says antigen binding and internalization were retained. Whether payload per antibody translated into more payload delivered in vivo is not quantified in the abstract.

#### Enhancing the efficacy of siRNA Antibody Oligonucleotide Conjugates (AOCs) through chemical design — PREPRINT
[DOI:10.64898/2026.09.23.753708](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR1326288) · bioRxiv · 2026-09-23 · not peer reviewed

DAR>1 siRNA-antibody conjugates lose potency. A single C16 lipid on the siRNA improved silencing over the unmodified AOC and restored activity at DAR2. At an equivalent siRNA dose (1 mg/kg), the DAR2-C16 AOC needed half the antibody of the DAR1-C16 AOC for comparable knockdown. A charge-balancing ionizable linker (CBIL) increased silencing in heart and skeletal muscle without a matching rise in liver activity. Percent knockdown values and the model are not reported in the abstract.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** loading. It gives a concrete chemical fix, lipid on the payload plus charge-balancing linker, for the DAR>1 potency penalty, and it doubles siRNA per antibody. It also gives an extrahepatic shift.

### Carrier formats (antibody-displayed nanoparticles)

#### Click-Chemistry Functionalized Lipid Nanoparticles for In Vitro and In Vivo Transfection of Primary Human CD4+ T Cells
[PMID:42764379](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764379/) · Adv Healthc Mater · 2026-09-20

A DBCO-azide click approach attaches a high-affinity CD4 nanobody to LNPs. Optimized CD4-targeted LNPs transfected up to 48% of non-activated CD4+ T cells in a PBMC co-culture (in vitro). They delivered CRISPR-Cas9 components to activated CD4+ T cells with up to 32% gene editing (in vitro). In humanized NSG-SGM3 mice they selectively associated with and transfected circulating and bone-marrow human CD4+ T cells with minimal murine off-target activity; in vivo percentages are not reported in abstract. The "highest efficiency reported so far" is the authors' claim.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** uptake. A nanobody on an LNP converts a liver-cleared carrier into one that loads resting lymphocytes, which is a direct uptake-per-targeting-unit result. The comparator against untargeted LNP is not given in the abstract.

#### Dual-targeted nanoparticles enable the ex vivo generation of cytotoxic and phagocytic CAR effector cells — PREPRINT
[DOI:10.64898/2026.09.21.753074](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.21.753074) · bioRxiv · 2026-09-22 · not peer reviewed

A new anti-CD222 antibody (CLCM08) was co-displayed with anti-CD5 on LNPs carrying CAR mRNA. Versus non-conjugated LNPs, this "substantially enhanced" CAR-monocyte and CAR-T generation from primary cells, and anti-CD222 LNPs gave the largest phagocytic gain in monocytes. The work is ex vivo only, and fold-change and percent values are not reported in abstract.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** uptake and valency. Co-display of two internalizing targeting antibodies addresses two cell types on one carrier. The abstract says CD222 "facilitates internalization", which makes it a candidate uptake receptor, but no number supports the size of the gain.

#### Supramolecular Pretargeting Lipid Nanoparticles Based on Host-Guest Recognition Potentiate Cancer Immunotherapy by Reprogramming Tumor Microenvironment
[PMID:42839730](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42839730/) · Angew Chem Int Ed · 2026-10-06

Pre-dosed adamantane-conjugated anti-PD-L1 antibody primes tumors to capture later-infused cucurbit[7]uril-functionalized LNPs carrying circRNA for IL-2-Fc. The authors report improved intratumoral retention and cellular uptake and suppressed tumor growth with longer survival. The abstract gives no fold-enrichment, %ID/g, or survival numbers ("not reported in abstract"). The antitumor benefit is confounded by the NLG919-modified lipid and the cytokine cargo, so the pretargeting contribution to delivery is not isolated in the abstract.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** transit and loading. The antibody is used as a docking site, not a payload carrier, so each antibody can recruit many LNPs. This is a format lever, but unquantified here.

## Also noted (score 3)

- **Trastuzumab deruxtecan kills skin and uveal melanoma in vivo by HER2-independent payload release** (PREPRINT) — [DOI:10.64898/2026.10.09.757939](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.09.757939) — ERBB2-knockout xenografts responded similarly to T-DXd initially; melanoma cells took up little trastuzumab in vitro, and extracellular cathepsin L may cleave the linker outside cells. Cleavable-linker payload can act without antibody internalization.
- **GPCR antagonism via rewiring of receptor trafficking and degradation** — [PMID:42816619](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42816619/) — TfR1 bispecifics (GTACs) drive GPCR endocytosis and lysosomal degradation, with more than one to two orders of magnitude greater potency than conventional antibody antagonists. Live-cell trafficking design rules may transfer to TfR1 shuttles. Cargo is degradation, not delivery.
- **Bevacizumab-Functionalized Exosome-Associated Chitosan Nanocomposite for Temozolomide Delivery** — [PMID:42828315](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42828315/) — antibody-displayed vesicle carrier with 68.50 ± 2.15% TMZ entrapment and 15.96 ± 0.18 wt% loading, with 74.9 ± 2.9% retained VEGF binding. Biodistribution and pharmacokinetics were not done; the abstract itself calls for them.
- **TROP2-Targeting Chimeras (TRTACs)** — [PMID:42801692](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42801692/) — ~30 kDa nanobody-nanobody fusion degrades EGFR, HER2, and PD-L1 preferentially in tumor cells; the HER2 TRTAC-drug conjugate reduced SKBR3 viability by 50% at 0.2 nM. No comparison to a non-degrading conjugate is reported in abstract.
- **Computational framework-guided engineering of microbial transglutaminase enables site-specific conjugation of Trastuzumab** — [PMID:42809913](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42809913/) — M3-MTGase-Sm (R48K/G283S/S303G) has 3.6-fold the activity of wild type and conjugates Q295 despite N297 glycan hindrance. Only probes and PEG were tested; the authors state it is not validated with cytotoxic payloads.
- **Catamers: Multi-specific therapeutics that concatenate individual warheads on a DNA scaffold** (PREPRINT) — [DOI:10.64898/2026.09.23.753027](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR1327404) — concept paper with only molecular simulation; a modular multi-warhead DNA scaffold (PSMA binder, deruxtecan, cleavage site, half-life extender). Not antibody-based, and the advantages are untested by the authors' own statement.
- **Nanobody-Functionalized AAV achieves Promoter-independent Neuronal Targeting in the CNS** (PREPRINT) — [DOI:10.64898/2026.09.18.752817](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR1325030) — anti-mGluR5 nanobody on an HSPG-ablated AAV-DJ capsid gave an eight-fold higher proportion of transduced hippocampal neurons than wild-type AAV-DJ at equivalent titers (mouse, stereotactic injection). Local injection, so it says nothing about transit. Vector, not antibody cargo.
- **Modular Lysosome-Targeting Platform Based on a Multivalent DNA Framework** — [PMID:42766830](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42766830/) — tetrahedral DNA scaffold gives multivalent aptamer display, "substantially improved internalization" and ~71% degradation at 100 nM after 24 h, compared with flexible and monovalent systems. Internalization fold-change is not reported in abstract. Valency lever, degradation cargo.
- **Rituximab-Conjugated DART Nanoformulation for CD20-Targeted Drug Delivery in NHL** — [PMID:42764913](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764913/) — rituximab-PLGA-PEG nanoparticles with 7-8% paclitaxel loading showed CD20-dependent association versus IgG-DART. Cell-line and xenograft evidence only; the authors say PK and biodistribution are still needed.
- **A bispecific antibody targeting a membrane-proximal ASGPR1 region promotes TNF-α recruitment and uptake** (PREPRINT) — [DOI:10.64898/2026.09.11.750848](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.11.750848) — in HepG2 cultures, supernatant TNF-α signal fell ~60% over 90 min, with uptake into LysoTracker-positive compartments. Binding is at ASGPR1 residues 62-100, outside the carbohydrate-recognition domain. Endocytic receptor choice insight; in vitro only.
