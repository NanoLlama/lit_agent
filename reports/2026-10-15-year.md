## Top findings (score 5)

### Bispecific antibodies that engage pIgR enrich in mucosa in cynomolgus monkeys
[PMID:42829333](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42829333/) · MAbs · 2026-10-03

The authors compared pIgR RNA and protein expression across human, cynomolgus and mouse tissues and found cyno similar to human. Anti-pIgR bispecifics cleared rapidly from serum and were 5-10-fold enriched in bronchoalveolar lavage fluid compared with normal IgG in cynomolgus monkeys. The measurement is in lavage fluid; the abstract gives no tissue-level or GI-mucosa number.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** first non-human-primate evidence that a pIgR arm raises IgG transit from blood to an epithelial lumen, which is a transit lever independent of the brain shuttles.

### Enhancing the efficacy of siRNA Antibody Oligonucleotide Conjugates (AOCs) through chemical design
[DOI:10.64898/2026.09.23.753708](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR1326288) · bioRxiv · 2026-09-23 · **PREPRINT**

A single C16 lipid on the siRNA restored the potency that is normally lost when going from DAR1 to DAR2 AOCs. At an equivalent siRNA dose of 1 mg/kg, the DAR2-C16 AOC needed half the antibody of the DAR1-C16 AOC for comparable knockdown. A charge-balancing ionizable linker (CBIL) shifted silencing toward heart and skeletal muscle relative to liver. Knockdown percentages and the CBIL fold shift are not reported in abstract.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** a direct fix for the DAR>1 potency penalty, which lets payload per antibody go up without losing activity.

### A binding-to-release strategy for targeted anticancer drug delivery
[PMID:42649302](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42649302/) · Nature · 2026-08-26

Phosphorus(V)-phenol exchange (PhoPEx) chemistry releases payload by a nucleophile in the binding pocket rather than after endocytosis. In mouse tumour models, the FAP-targeted binding-to-release small-molecule conjugate gave 5.9-fold higher tumour MMAE exposure (AUC0-120 h) than the internalization-dependent small-molecule conjugate, matching the internalization-dependent ADC. Tumour-to-blood ratio was 14.7-fold higher than the internalization-dependent small-molecule conjugate and 3.6-fold higher than the ADC. The headline comparator is a small-molecule conjugate, not an antibody.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** it removes the need for efficient receptor-mediated endocytosis, so poorly internalizing targets become usable for payload delivery (uptake route, not loading or transit).

### pIgR Stem Zone-Targeted Nanobodies as Apical-to-Basolateral Carriers for Inhaled Biologic Delivery Across Mucosal Barriers
[PMID:42496279](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42496279/) · Antibodies (Basel) · 2026-06-23

Phage display gave four nanobodies to the pIgR stem domain (HDX-MS epitope: residues 578-612, distinct from the dIgA site). Two leads showed apical-to-basolateral transport in Transwell assays and in vivo fluorescence imaging; transport was not quantified in the abstract. Inhaled nanobody-reslizumab bispecifics at one-tenth the intraperitoneal reslizumab dose were effective in a murine asthma model, while reslizumab alone at equivalent concentrations failed.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** a roughly 10-fold dose-sparing inferred from efficacy, so delivered mass was not measured, and it supports the pIgR stem domain as a transit handle.

### Chemically Masked aPD-L1-Sialidase Conjugate for Tumor-Specific Desialylation and Immunotherapy
[PMID:42241306](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42241306/) · Angew Chem Int Ed Engl · 2026-06-04

A nanobody-sialidase conjugate carries a PEG mask (non-canonical amino acid, removed by tumour MMP2). Compared with the unmasked control, the masked conjugate (CAPS) had a 144-fold longer systemic half-life and 9.3-fold higher tumour-site accumulation in mice. The abstract does not separate how much of the accumulation comes from the half-life extension.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** shows that a cleavable PEG mask can raise tumour accumulation of an enzyme cargo on a nanobody by exposure and retention (transit), at the price of a conditional activation step.

### Utilizing biorecognition to prime tumors for enhanced nanomedicine delivery of alpha therapies
[PMID:41687816](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41687816/) · J Control Release · 2026-02-11

An anti-EGFR/anti-PEG bispecific antibody primes tumours, and a PEG-based 212Pb nanocarrier is then given. Pretargeting more than tripled tumour retention of the 212Pb-nanomedicine over the untargeted nanomaterial in an EGFR+ breast cancer xenograft (SPECT and gamma counting of excised organs), and increased receptor-mediated internalization in vitro.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** a modular bispecific-plus-nanocarrier route that decouples loading (on the nanocarrier) from targeting (on the antibody).

### High-payload trastuzumab-drug conjugates achieved through hydrophilic Y-shaped pendant PEG linkers
[PMID:41558564](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41558564/) · Int J Biol Macromol · 2026-01-18

A Y-shaped PEG12 pendant linker allows trastuzumab-VC-pABC-MMAE at DAR 8, against the DAR 4 ceiling the authors cite for approved ADCs, with the payload structure unchanged. The abstract reports "excellent tumor accumulation and in vivo efficacy" without a number.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** doubles payload per antibody with an unmodified clinical payload; the tumour-accumulation gain is not reported in abstract.

### HER2 Antibody-Nanogel Conjugates: A High-Capacity, Antibody-Sparing Alternative to Antibody-Drug Conjugates
[PMID:41433257](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41433257/) · Nano Lett · 2025-12-23

HER2 antibody-nanogel conjugates with covalently encapsulated DM1 matched T-DM1 efficacy in a BT-474 xenograft while requiring 4-fold less antibody; in vitro cytotoxicity was comparable to T-DM1. The DAR itself is not reported in abstract, and the authors call the work proof of concept.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** raises cargo per antibody through the carrier format instead of linker chemistry, with no payload modification.

### Transferrin receptor-binding blood-brain barrier shuttle enhances brain delivery and plaque-clearing efficacy of a therapeutic anti-Aβ antibody
[PMID:41310626](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41310626/) · Fluids Barriers CNS · 2025-11-27

A mouse TfR1-binding Fab fused to aducanumab (TfR1-Adu) gave higher and more homogeneous brain distribution than unmodified aducanumab after acute and chronic dosing (light-sheet imaging of cleared hemispheres in APP/PS1 mice). A five-fold lower dose of TfR1-Adu reduced plaque burden comparably to high-dose chronic aducanumab. The brain-delivery fold-change is not reported in abstract.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** measured in tissue, not inferred from plasma, and the five-fold dose-sparing is a concrete benchmark for a TfR1 shuttle on a therapeutic antibody.

### Polyphenol-Mediated Antibody Functionalization of Titanium Peroxide Nanoparticles for Cancer Cell Targeting
[PMID:41208811](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41208811/) · ACS Appl Bio Mater · 2025-11-10

Tannic-acid grafting of anti-CD44 antibody onto PAATiOx nanoparticles gave ~2-fold higher binding to CD44-expressing cells than unmodified nanoparticles. Internalization was 2.4-fold (MIAPaCa-2) and 6.5-fold (MDA-MB-231) higher in vitro, and in vivo tumour accumulation was ~2-fold higher (p < 0.05) than unmodified nanoparticles. The comparator is an untargeted nanoparticle.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** a simple one-pot way to put antibody on a nanoparticle that raises uptake; it says little about antibody-intrinsic delivery.

### A multivalent nanobody-drug conjugate to prevent and treat influenza virus infections
[PMID:41183201](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41183201/) · Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A · 2025-11-03

A nanobody against mouse kappa light chain (VHHkappa) carries 1, 2 or 4 site-specific zanamivir copies. The 4-copy adduct (VHHkappa-Zan4) was ~10-fold more potent than the single-copy adduct in protecting mice from influenza A infection, and intranasal dosing protected against a lethal challenge. The ~10-fold is potency in protection, not measured payload delivered.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** direct evidence that more payload copies per nanobody, matched to the tetrameric target, raises effect (loading and avidity), in mice only.

### Remodeling Cellular Uptake via Inducing Clustering of Urokinase Receptors in Hydrogel Microspheres for Alleviating Cartilage Senescence
[PMID:41117256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41117256/) · Adv Healthc Mater · 2025-10-21

Anti-uPAR antibody on cationic liposomes carrying Sirt6 plasmid induced uPAR clustering and enhanced clathrin-mediated uptake: 1.7-2-fold higher uptake in vitro and 1.5-fold in vivo versus Lipo-Sirt6 (no antibody).

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** a mechanistic uptake lever (antibody-driven receptor clustering to rescue impaired endocytosis in senescent cells), though the comparator is an untargeted liposome.

### Targeted lipid nanoparticles containing IL-10 mRNA improve outcomes in experimental intracerebral hemorrhage
[PMID:41088400](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41088400/) · J Neuroinflammation · 2025-10-14

VCAM-antibody-conjugated LNPs distributed to the brain ~4x more than nonspecific LNPs, and cargo expression in the brain was 10x higher, in mice with intracerebral hemorrhage. Treatment cut hematoma size ~69% at 72 h and improved motor behaviour ~65%. The 10x figure is expression, not cargo mass.

**Why it matters for increasing cargo delivery:** targeting an inflamed endothelium rather than crossing the BBB; shows the size of the gain from antibody-LNP versus nonspecific LNP in vivo.

Also [PMID:42620052](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42620052/) (**PREPRINT**; supramolecular site-specific MMAE ADC gave better survival than cysteine ADCs with higher loading in a rat model), [PMID:41391917](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41391917/) (iminothiolane "phantom DAR" artifact in lysine conjugation), [PMID:42333517](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42333517/) (DAR 8 cetuximab-I21, >400-fold selectivity) and [PMID:41994867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41994867/) (TfR1 AOC, ~75% DUX4-regulated gene reduction 8 weeks after one dose in FSHD mice).

**Trafficking and endosomal escape (37).** The largest share (roughly 15 by my count) are lysosome-targeting or degrader chimeras, where the readout is degradation rather than delivered cargo, so they inform routing but not loading. The ones that matter for delivery: [PMID:42272747](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42272747/) (antibody-LNP internalisation in primary T and B cells did not predict mRNA translation), [PMID:42173446](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42173446/) (receptor internalization, not abundance, determines CD7 tLNP delivery), [PMID:42167230](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42167230/) (AKR1C1 blocks NECTIN4-ADC internalization and exports ADC in EVs), [DOI:10.64898/2026.05.15.725439](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.15.725439) (**PREPRINT**; FLOT2 loss increases T-DM1 internalization), [DOI:10.64898/2026.06.22.733843](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.22.733843) (**PREPRINT**; EGFR:LGR5 bispecific ADC 100- to 1000-fold more potent than monospecific ADC, in vitro), and [PMID:42286928](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42286928/) (ADC internalization and lysosomal degradation are temporally uncoupled).

**Carrier formats (49).** Antibody- or nanobody-decorated LNPs, liposomes, EVs, polymersomes and nanoparticles. Typical limitation: targeting shown by imaging or efficacy, cargo not quantified, often in vitro or zebrafish only. Exceptions and negatives worth knowing: [DOI:10.64898/2026.07.02.736109](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.02.736109) (**PREPRINT**; DSPE-PEG-mAb sheds from LNPs within 1 h in blood, PCL-b-PEG anchor holds antibody over 24 h), [PMID:42310937](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42310937/) (nanobody-displaying EVs only slightly increased bone marrow delivery and raised off-target accumulation), [DOI:10.64898/2026.05.21.726796](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.21.726796) (**PREPRINT**; hydrogel depot gave 78% relative bioavailability of SC trastuzumab-ADC in primates, 7.6-fold lower peak free DM1 than IV), [PMID:42674453](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42674453/) (148-fold in vitro beta-cell transfection from composition screening; antibody/nanobody added over 2-fold pancreatic expression) and [PMID:41845388](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41845388/) (scFv-exosomes 79.3% tumour-cell uptake vs 47.9% liposomes after nebulization). [PMID:41881071](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41881071/) is in this bucket and flagged for a human read (see Housekeeping).
